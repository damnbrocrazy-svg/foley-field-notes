    THE NOTES BEHIND THE NOISE

    Written like a recipe.
    Played like a world.

    Each sound is a small set of tones and filtered noise: a material, a gesture, and a handful of synthesis layers. Scene cues reference those recipes, so changing a recipe can change a whole scene.

    The collection comes from Sanity. Recipes pass through draft, review, and approval before appearing here. An edit invalidates the old approval. The starting collection is clearly labelled as an automated demonstration seed; it does not claim a human review.

    Use a preset, change its cue times and levels, or make your own arrangement. No login or microphone access is needed to play.
